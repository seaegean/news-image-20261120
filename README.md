A one-image newspaper front page summing up the world news of Friday 20 November 2026, drawn as "The Daily Ledger". The image was built in code with Python and Pillow — masthead, dateline, headlines, columns and footer laid out by hand — so every word on the page is set type, not a photograph.

## What the front page shows

The lead story is Russia's overnight barrage on Ukraine: 404 drones and more than 110 missiles, three bridges across the Dnipro in Kyiv struck, eight people killed, and two Russian missiles coming down inside Poland, a NATO member — the gravest spillover of the war so far.

The markets box carries the bearish Anthropic angle. ANT, the record $81 billion IPO that closed its debut at $97.17, fell 8.5% to $88.91 on its second day, after touching $83.05 — five cents above its $83 offer price. Anyone who bought at the $111.50 open is down a fifth in a day. The wider sell-off ran with it: the S&P 500 fell 3.6%, a seventh straight loss that put it in correction, the Nasdaq dropped 5.1%, and Bitcoin slipped below $60,000.

The rest of the page covers the US Senate voting 51–48 to block the Iran war-powers resolution, Estonia closing its road crossings with Russia from 1 December for at least six months, a Houthi missile damaging Riyadh's tallest tower before the Saudi-led coalition struck back at Hodeidah port, GTA VI selling about 24.6 million copies in a day for $2.36 billion, and JPMorgan, Visa and Mastercard confirming customer data was exposed in a third-party vendor breach claimed by the extortion group n0n.

Every story on the page was checked against live reporting on 21 November 2026, and only stories that happened or were first reported on 20 November made the cut.
